About Me

I am an administrative officer with 10 years of experience streamlining processes and operations. I enjoy building organized, supportive environments where teams can do their best work and organizations can reach their goals.

I coordinate meetings, events, and activities across multiple departments, and I use data analysis tools such as Excel and SPSS to improve results and productivity. From 2018 to 2025 I managed the administrative department for the Electoral Commission of Ghana in the Central Region, supporting voter registration, training, and national elections.

I am now pursuing an MS in Technology at Eastern Illinois University. Outside of work, I write poetry, mentor students, and advocate for gender equality and inclusion.

Interests: Information Technology, Administration and Management, Public Sector Administration, Management Information Systems, Data Analysis, Research.
